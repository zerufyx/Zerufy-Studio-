## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS generated by a zero-dependency Node script (`scripts/build.mjs`), deployed on GitHub Pages. Same stack as every client project (Zerufy, AMH Store, S91). The owner chose to keep this stack; no framework, no build tooling beyond Node. Libraries only by CDN, only when they earn their place.

## Users

Owners of small businesses who sell through Instagram, TikTok and WhatsApp and still send prices by DM or photo: resellers and clothing/accessory/perfume shops, food trucks and restaurants, car dealerships and lots, and service businesses. Mostly Spanish-speaking. They arrive on a phone, from a link in a bio, a story, a WhatsApp message or a QR code. The owner wants to reach as many markets as possible (Orlando/Florida first, and Latin America, e.g. Venezuela), not one city.

Their job: decide in a minute or two whether Zerufy Studio can give their business a real digital presence, and start a WhatsApp conversation.

## Product Purpose

Zerufy Studio builds the digital infrastructure of small businesses: websites, digital menus, digital catalogs with cart and orders by WhatsApp, online stores, and admin panels the owner runs by themselves. Growth path: e-commerce, custom systems, automation, mobile apps.

Success for the site: a visitor opens a real example, trusts it, and writes on WhatsApp or sends the "Crear mi proyecto" form.

## Positioning

"Yo te doy la herramienta, tú manejas tu negocio." Every client gets a working product plus a panel to change prices, photos, stock and availability from the phone, without depending on the studio. All clients run on one shared platform (one Supabase project, one store per business), so setup is fast and cheap compared with agencies and page builders. The work is real and live: people can open S91, Zerufy and AMH on their own phone.

## Operating Context

- Leads close on WhatsApp (+1 407 283 3785). The form saves to Supabase (`leads` table) and always offers WhatsApp as the path.
- Traffic is mobile, arriving from social apps and QR codes; the site must feel fast and app-like on a phone.
- The owner also works a 9-to-5; the site should keep selling without him.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Every project is delivered with an admin panel: the owner makes their own changes; the studio provides support and maintenance (owner's rule, 2026-09-28). The site must say this clearly.
- No online card payments are offered. Orders and bookings arrive by WhatsApp; the owner handles payment.
- Pages per business type live at /negocios/ (restaurantes, tiendas, concesionarios, citas), in the pitch format: pains, what we give, live panel demo, how it sells, you vs. us.

- Prices are NOT shown on the site. Pricing is discussed on WhatsApp.
- Vertical-specific offers exist (reventa, comida, concesionarios, servicios); all verticals are targeted equally.
- Spanish first. An English version is a later step.
- Must not break or restyle the client projects or their Supabase data from this repo.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Zerufy Studio. Wordmark style "Zerufy Studio." with red "Zerufy" and red period.
- Existing pitch identity (binding): near-black backgrounds, a single red accent `#e8352a`, Bebas Neue for display, Montserrat for text, direct second-person voice, short punchy sentences, one emphasized red word per headline. Reference: the owner's "Zerufy Studio · Catálogo digital para tu lote" pitch file.
- Direction (2026-09-28, owner brief "Zerufy Studio 2.0"): a premium, experimental creative digital studio site, not a generic agency. Positioning line: "Creamos experiencias digitales." All copy in Spanish.
- Shape rule from the pitch: pill buttons, 16px cards, 12px inputs.
- Voice: confident, friendly, a little confrontational ("¿Todavía vendes así?"), no corporate jargon.

## Product Principles

1. Show, don't claim: real, openable work beats any promise.
2. Everything leads to WhatsApp in one tap.
3. The client stays in control of their business; the studio gives the tool.
4. Nothing on the page that does not help someone decide or act.
5. Phone first, always.
